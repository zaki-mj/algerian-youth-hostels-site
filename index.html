Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 07, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Algerian Youth Hostels mobile application ("we", "us", or "our").

We do not collect, store, process, or share any personal information from users.

The app is a simple directory that displays publicly available contact information for youth hostels in Algeria. No user accounts, logins, analytics, device identifiers, location data, IP addresses, or any other personal or device data are collected — even anonymously.

We use no third-party services (including Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Google Analytics, or any tracking SDKs) that transmit data off-device. The app operates entirely locally on your device with no data transmission to us or any other party.

No Data Collection

We do not collect:

  • Personal identifiers (name, email, phone, etc.)
  • Device information (ID, model, OS version, etc.)
  • Usage data, analytics, crash reports, or logs
  • Location, IP address, or any other metadata
  • Any other data — period.

No Data Sharing

Since no data is collected, nothing is shared with third parties, service providers, advertisers, or anyone else.

Scope and Limitations

This app only provides contact information for youth hostels in Algeria as a public directory service. We do not facilitate bookings, verify information, or participate in any transactions or communications. We are not responsible or liable for any actions, agreements, disputes, or outcomes that occur after users contact hostels using the provided information.

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from minors. It is intended for general audiences.
